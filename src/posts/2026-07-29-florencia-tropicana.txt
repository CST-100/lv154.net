a transcription of Emperor X's Florencia Tropicana

heard chad play this song live for the first time at The Echo
in LA on July 25, 2026 and can't get it out of my head. i
believe this is the first tab of it ever, only about 21 years
late. haha

{g}Florencia Tropicana{/g}
{d}Tectonic Membrane / Thin Strip on an Edgeless Platform{/d}
by Emperor X / Chad R. Matheny, 2004

TUNING: EADGBe
KEY: A
there seem to be two loops to this, A and B.

LOOP A goes: D, A, B/F#, F#m, E
count: (D) 1-2-3, (A)(B/F#)(F#m) the same, E 1-2-3-4

LOOP B goes: G, A, F#m,  G

where:
D   = xx0232
A   = 002220
B/F#= 224442
F#m = 244222
E   = 022100
G   = 320003

{d}note: B/F# could also be 224422, which would be F#sus4. unsure.{/d}
